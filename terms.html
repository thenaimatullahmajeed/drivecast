The operator is responsible for verifying folder access, the target channel, upload metadata and video privacy before enabling uploads.

Scheduling and reliability

Schedules depend on GitHub Actions availability, account quotas, valid Google authorization and video transfer/processing time. Exact upload or publication times and a minimum number of successful uploads are not guaranteed.

The uploader reserves up to two attempts per client folder per New York calendar day. Failed or interrupted attempts also consume a slot. It avoids automatically retrying files in Processing or Failed because an interrupted upload may already exist on YouTube. Operators must review uncertain outcomes before manually requeuing files. This protection depends on using one worker/repository per folder and preserving its state; copied files and manual resets may cause separate uploads.

Removing authorization does not automatically remove videos or stored configuration; see the privacy policy’s deletion instructions.
