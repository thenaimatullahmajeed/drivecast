Terms of service

DriveCast is an operator-run local tool connecting a configured Drive or supported Instagram source to an authorized YouTube channel and optional Facebook Page. Use it under these conditions and the privacy policy.

Authorization and content rights

Connect only accounts, repositories, folders and channels that you own or are authorized to manage. Obtain permission to republish each creator's videos, including necessary music, copyright and likeness rights. A public Instagram username does not establish those rights. Verify the exact destination channel and metadata before enabling a pipeline.

Use official authorization screens. Do not request client passwords, bypass private-account restrictions, or use the tool for infringement, spam or fraud. Comply with YouTube's terms and applicable Google, Meta and GitHub policies. Multiple GitHub connections provide explicit ownership and isolation; they do not authorize quota evasion or unlimited free execution. GitHub-hosted workloads remain subject to GitHub Actions terms and provider eligibility.

Availability and reliability

Schedules depend on runner availability, valid authorization, API quotas, account eligibility and transfer/processing time. Instagram Business Discovery may not provide a requested account or all historical media/download URLs. The tool does not recover lost account credentials or guarantee media availability, exact publication times, successful uploads, SEO rankings or monetization.

The worker reserves the configured maximum attempts per destination per New York calendar day (default two), including YouTube tests and failed/interrupted attempts. Normal workflows request Public; tests are private YouTube-only. Polling intervals govern when new videos are discovered; they do not guarantee instant publication. Each destination processes independently and may publish at a different time. It records a reservation before upload and quarantines uncertain outcomes. Drive files left in Processing/Failed and ledger entries needing review are never automatically requeued. Review YouTube Studio before any manual retry.

Duplicate protection depends on preserving the original state ledger and source IDs, using one writer per pipeline and respecting pinned channel/source routing. Independently configured tools, copied source files, deleted records or manual resets can cause separate uploads. The operator must protect and review this state.

Security, access and ending use

Protect the Recovery Password and accounts; restrict repository/workflow access. GitHub repository owners retain control of their repositories and workflows even though the local panel has one operator. Platform authorization can expire or be revoked. Losing the Recovery Password can prevent decrypting backups. Pause workflows and revoke access to end use; then follow the privacy policy's data deletion steps. Revocation does not automatically delete uploaded videos or history.
