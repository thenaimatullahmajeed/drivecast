Google Drive → YouTube

Your video queue,
on schedule.

DriveCast uploads videos from a chosen Google Drive folder to a verified YouTube channel. Each client connects their own accounts, with a separate queue and private automation repository.

How your data is usedRead the setup guide →
  1. Add videos to DriveMP4, MOV or MKV files in the configured folder.
  2. Authorize Drive and YouTubeTwo separate Google approvals support Brand channels.
  3. Let the scheduled uploader runProcessing → Done, with the YouTube video ID recorded.

You decide what connects.

The operator configures a specific source folder and target channel. DriveCast verifies the channel ID before real uploads and processes videos oldest first.

Separate clients

Each client has a private GitHub repository, credentials and schedule. Drive and YouTube can be authorized by different Google accounts.

Conservative recovery

Files left in Processing or Failed are held for manual review. Uncertain uploads are never automatically started again.

A small daily queue

One video per scheduled run, with at most two reserved upload attempts per New York calendar day. Failed attempts also use a daily slot.

What this website does

This is DriveCast’s public information and policy website. Account connection happens in the operator’s local setup panel; scheduled video processing runs in private GitHub Actions repositories. This website does not accept Google credentials or video uploads.

Google authorization and any required verification must be completed before unattended operation. Publishing this website does not establish Google approval.

Access with your consent

Google Drive access reads and organizes queued files. YouTube upload access sends videos, while read-only channel access verifies the intended destination. You may revoke access through your Google Account.
